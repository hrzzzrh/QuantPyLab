# Research Framework

## 1. Initial Research Roadmap (Template)
Before any searching, generate this roadmap:
- **Research Goal**: Clear statement of the primary objective.
- **Core Investigation Pillars**:
  - Pillar 1 (e.g., Supply Chain Vulnerabilities): Why this matters + Key questions.
  - Pillar 2 (e.g., Regulatory Hurdles): Specific policies to check.
- **Proposed Keywords**: Top-level search terms in both CN and EN.
- **Success Criteria**: What evidence is needed to close the investigation?

## 2. Standard Report Structure

### Executive Summary
- **Core Thesis**: One-sentence summary of the finding.
- **Key Takeaways**: 3-5 bullet points.

### Background & Context
- Definition of the subject.
- Historical context or current state of play.

### Deep Dive Analysis
- **Dimension 1 (e.g., Market Size/Industry Structure)**
- **Dimension 2 (e.g., Competitive Moat/Technical Barriers)**
- **Dimension 3 (e.g., Financial Performance/Macro Impact)**

### Risk Assessment
- Potential headwinds or bull/bear case scenarios.

### Synthesis & Conclusion
- Final verdict or strategic recommendation.

### Sources & References
- Markdown list of [Title](URL) used in the research.

## 2. Search Protocols
- **Bilingual Strategy**: Always search in both Chinese and English for international topics (e.g., "HJT Solar Cell efficiency" + "HJT 电池片效率").
- **Date Filtering**: Prioritize results from the last 12-24 months unless historical context is needed.
- **Source Variety**: Combine official company IR pages, industry associations (e.g., CPCA), and top-tier financial news (e.g., Caixin, Bloomberg).

## 3. The Dynamic Research Loop
A high-quality deep research MUST NOT follow a fixed number of rounds. Instead, it must be driven by the **Information Sufficiency Principle**:

### Continuous Execution Loop:
1.  **Search & Fetch**: Use tools to gather data.
2.  **Synthesis-in-Progress**: Incorporate new info into a working mental model.
3.  **Gap Assessment**:
    -   What is still missing to prove/disprove the core thesis?
    -   What source is conflicting or weak?
4.  **Dynamic Decision**:
    -   **Drill**: If a new lead appears (e.g., a specific lawsuit or regulation), pursue it.
    -   **Diversify**: If data is all from one source, find alternative viewpoints.
    -   **Finalize**: If gaps are closed and objective is met, stop.

### Termination Criteria:
- Core metrics (market size, key players, financial ratios) are confirmed.
- Bull/Bear cases are both articulated with evidence.
- Both English and Chinese (where applicable) perspectives have been sampled.
- No high-value "Knowledge Gaps" remain.
