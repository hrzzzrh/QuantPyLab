# Data Confidence Guidelines

## Tier 1: Gold Standard (High Confidence)
- **Official Disclosures**: 10-K, 10-Q, Annual/Quarterly Reports from SEC or Exchange (SSE/SZSE).
- **Official Government Data**: NBS (National Bureau of Statistics), Central Bank (PBOC/Fed) reports.
- **Academic Research**: Peer-reviewed journals, white papers from established research institutes.

## Tier 2: Reliable (Medium-High Confidence)
- **Tier-1 Financial News**: Bloomberg, Reuters, Financial Times, Caixin, Wall Street Journal.
- **Industry Analyst Reports**: Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, CICC, Sinolink Securities (if verified by 2+ sources).
- **Industry Associations**: Reports from established bodies (e.g., CPCA for automotive, SEMI for semiconductors).

## Tier 3: Contextual (Low-Medium Confidence)
- **General News**: Reuters/AP/Xinhua (news-focused, may lack depth).
- **Public Forum & Community Data**: Snowball (Xueqiu), Reddit, Twitter (use for sentiment or anecdotal evidence only).
- **Blogs & Social Media**: Substack, WeChat Public Accounts (cross-check required).

## Handling Conflict
If sources disagree:
1. **Recency**: Prefer the latest data point (assuming it's Tier 1/2).
2. **Methodology**: Prefer the source that clearly explains its calculation method.
3. **Hierarchy**: Tier 1 always trumps Tier 2/3.
4. **Triangulation**: If conflict remains, find a 3rd independent source to break the tie.
